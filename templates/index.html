Why This Project Matters for Farmers

Traditional farming relies on guesswork. Precision agriculture with AI brings certainty and higher profitability.

❌ The Farmer's Challenge

  • Uncertain Yields: Inability to anticipate final harvest volume leads to poor pricing decisions.
  • Fertilizer Overuse / Underuse: Applying incorrect fertilizer quantities degrades soil health and increases cost.
  • Climate Vulnerability: Erratic monsoon rains and heatwaves create sudden, unexpected crop failures.
  • Credit & Storage Bottlenecks: Banks and grain warehouses require reliable yield projections for loans and storage allocation.

✅ The CropYield AI Solution

  • Accurate Predictions: Get expected harvest in Tonnes/Hectare and Quintals/Acre instantly.
  • Smart Agronomic Advisory: Automated warnings if rainfall or temperature threatens crop growth.
  • Nutrient Optimization: Tailored fertilizer balance checks according to agronomic benchmarks.
  • Printable Farmer Card: Generate an official 1-page summary for local agricultural officers and banks.
