Complete 6-Step Guide for Farmers

Everything you need to know from creating an account to predicting harvest and printing your card.

1
Free Farmer Access

Create Account & Log In

Before making predictions, you need a free farmer account so all your predictions are securely saved to your personal history.

📝 Easy Registration Steps:

  1. Click 📝 Register on the top right navigation bar.
  2. Enter your Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, and set a password.
  3. Select your State / Union Territory from all 28 states & 8 UTs of India.
  4. Enter your typical Farm Size (in Hectares or Acres).
  5. Click Register & Continue. You will be instantly logged in!
💡 Already have an account? Just click 🔑 Login and enter your email and password.
2
Core Feature

Fill In Your Farm & Crop Details

Go to the Predict Yield page. The form asks for real-world agricultural conditions:

🌾 1. Select Crop
Choose from {{ crops_count or 32 }}+ major crops including Rice, Wheat, Cotton, Sugarcane, Onion, Apple, Tea, Banana, Turmeric, Pulses, and more.
📍 2. State / Region
Select your farm location from all {{ states_count or 36 }} Indian States & Union Territories.
☀️ 3. Season & Soil
Choose your cropping season (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid, Whole Year) and soil type (Alluvial, Black, Clayey, Red, Sandy Loam, Laterite).
📐 4. Land Area
Enter your farmland size. Use the handy Ha / Acres toggle switch to type in whatever unit you prefer! (1 Ha ≈ 2.47 Acres).
🌧️ 5. Rainfall & Temperature
Enter expected seasonal rainfall in millimeters (mm) and average temperature in Celsius (°C).
🧪 6. Fertilizer & Pesticides
Enter total N-P-K fertilizer application in kg/ha and pesticide usage in kg/ha.
✨
Farmer Pro-Tip: Try One-Click Autofill!

Don't know your exact local rainfall or fertilizer dosage? Simply click any of the ✨ Quick Autofill Scenario buttons (e.g. "🌾 Punjab Wheat", "🌱 AP Rice", "🧅 Maharashtra Onion", "🍎 Kashmir Apple"). The form will automatically populate with optimal scientific values!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from farmers using the portal

❓ Is CropYield AI completely free to use?

Yes! CropYield AI is 100% free for all farmers, agronomists, and agricultural students across India. There are no fees or hidden charges.

❓ How accurate are the yield predictions?

Our machine learning model is trained on 8,960 official agro-climatic records calibrated with ICAR and Directorate of Economics and Statistics benchmarks, achieving an $R^2$ accuracy score of 98.82%.

❓ Can I show the Farmer Yield Card to bank officers for crop loans?

Yes! When you click "Print Farmer Yield Card", you get a clean 1-page summary detailing your crop, land acreage, and anticipated harvest volumes in Tonnes and Quintals, useful for credit estimation and storage bookings.

❓ Who can I call for emergency agricultural help?

You can call the Government of India Kisan Call Center toll-free at 1800-180-1551 (available in 22 languages from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily).
